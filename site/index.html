A few questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Barkenson is free to use.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the models during setup. Recording and transcription work offline afterwards.

How accurate are the speaker labels?

Short replies and overlapping voices can be mislabelled. You can rename speakers and check the recording.
